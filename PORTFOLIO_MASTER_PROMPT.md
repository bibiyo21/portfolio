26. Full-screen parallax slide presentation — high-priority design direction

- Preserve native mouse wheel, trackpad, touch, Page Up/Down, Space, Home/End, and arrow-key behavior. Do not trap the wheel or implement brittle scroll hijacking.
- Use CSS Scroll Snap plus IntersectionObserver to identify the active scene and trigger its animation.

- Keep normal browser scrolling and direct section anchors working.
- Include a “Skip to content” link and a clear Contact shortcut.

- Midground: cards, diagrams, lines, frames; moderate movement.
- Foreground: headings, labels, CTA buttons; subtle entrance/exit motion.

During transitions:

- Preserve logical DOM order, semantic headings, accessible controls, and visible focus states.

- Avoid flashing, rapid movement, or motion likely to cause discomfort.
Performance
