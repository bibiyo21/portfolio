Ibrahem Sotejo: logo, favicon and typography

Three original SVG concepts, favicon candidates derived from each, and the proposed type system. The live portfolio still uses its current branding until you choose.

Recommendation: Concept A (IS monogram) with the Space Grotesk wordmark. It is the most distinctive of the three, stays legible at 16 px as a favicon, and pairs naturally with the type system. Concept C is the safest alternative. Concept B is the weakest: it reads as a generic org chart.
How to choose

1. Concepts

A · Recommended

IS monogram

A straight I and a stepped, round-cornered S built on one grid: same cap height, same stroke weight, same gap. The S's bottom bar runs right up to the I's edge, so the two letters nest on a single baseline. The squared structure suggests architecture and precision while the S's rounded corners keep it from reading as a 5.

  • Strong at small sizes: the favicon is the same shape.
  • One colour per variant, so it prints and embosses cleanly.
  • Honest note: overlapping the letters (a true interlock) read as TS, JS, LS or $ in testing, so the letters stay separate and nest instead.
B

Architecture layers

Three offset plates joined by a thin connector and a peach node: layers of a system and the links between them. It echoes the layered plates in the hero, so the logo and the page tell the same story.

  • Directly says "architecture", no letters needed.
  • Does not carry the initials, so it is less personal.
  • Reads somewhat like a flowchart or Gantt icon, which weakens distinctiveness.
C

Typographic wordmark

Tracked Space Grotesk capitals with the two initials picked out in burgundy, a thin rule with a short burgundy lead, and the role in mono. The compact "IS" is plain Space Grotesk Bold with a peach underline.

  • Most refined and the quietest, very editorial.
  • Depends entirely on the typeface, so there is no ownable shape.
  • The long wordmark is too wide for the mobile header; the compact IS would stand in there.

Weights actually loaded: Space Grotesk 500/600/700, JetBrains Mono 400/500, Inter variable.

Design consequence to confirm: the portfolio currently pairs Fraunces (a warm serif with italic accents) with Inter. Moving headings to Space Grotesk makes the site feel more technical and less editorial, and the italic accent in the hero ("Building Possibilities.") would become colour-only. It fits the "technology-oriented" brief, but it is a visible change to the design language.
